Roof Capacity & PV Design Decision

The property's theoretical solar requirement is approximately 55 kW, which would require approximately 73 × 750 W panels under the original assessment.

However, the available roof area cannot accommodate the full theoretical array. The practical installation has therefore been limited to 45 solar modules.

With the selected 615 W panels, the available roof capacity provides a total installed PV capacity of 27.675 kWp.

To improve energy availability outside solar production hours, the system incorporates 100 kWh of lithium storage and night-time grid charging. This allows the battery bank to be replenished from the grid during scheduled periods when required.

System Operation

  • Solar power supplies the property's daytime demand and charges the battery bank.
  • Battery storage supplies loads during power interruptions and scheduled discharge periods.
  • Grid charging is introduced during the night to replenish battery energy when required.
  • The three-inverter architecture provides a combined 48 kVA conversion capacity.
  • System operation should prioritize essential loads during constrained solar or battery conditions.
ENGINEERING BOUNDARY

Roof Area Controls PV Capacity

The 55 kW theoretical PV requirement is not the installed system size. The quoted PV system is intentionally limited to the available roof capacity: 45 × 615 W = 27.675 kWp.
