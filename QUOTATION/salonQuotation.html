Proposed Premium Barbing Salon / Commercial Energy Solution

36kVA Parallel Hybrid Solar Power & Energy Management System

A professionally engineered commercial hybrid solar energy system designed for a high-demand barbing salon in Abuja. The proposed system comprises three 12kVA hybrid inverters configured in parallel, a 45kWh lithium energy storage bank and a 22.32kWp Jinko photovoltaic array. The system is designed to support 10 air-conditioning units, barbing machines and other salon equipment, refrigeration, televisions and complete salon lighting. It operates as a hybrid system synchronized with the utility grid (NEPA/DisCo), with solar given priority as the primary energy source. Battery energy provides backup and load support, while grid import is automatically reduced and used only when solar generation and available battery capacity are insufficient.
