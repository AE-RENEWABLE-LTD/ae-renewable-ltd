ENGINEERING RULE

The required battery energy is calculated from the backup load and the allowable Depth of Discharge.

Battery Required Energy = (Backup Load × Backup Time) ÷ DoD

BILL OF QUANTITIES ENGINE

Engineering Result → Commercial Schedule

ARDE must never generate a BOQ directly from raw user input. Every quantity appearing in the BOQ must originate from the final validated engineering configuration.

ENGINEERING RESULT → BOQ BUILD → COST CALCULATION → VALIDATION → CONFIRMED BOQ
BOQ STATUS VALIDATED Only validated engineering results may produce the final commercial schedule.
BOQ Generation Logic
01 Engineering Status engineeringStatus === "VALID"
02 Read Final Equipment Panel + Inverter + Battery
03 Read Validated PV Configuration Strings + MPPT + Remaining Panels
04 Read Protection Configuration AC + DC + SPD + Battery Protection
05 Build Material Schedule Cables + MC4 + Pipe + Accessories
06 Calculate Cost Quantity × Unit Price
07 Validate BOQ Prices + Quantities + Totals
BLOCK RULE If engineering validation fails, ARDE must not generate a confirmed BOQ.
